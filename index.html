Hours Ledger — free to use, free to pass on. Your week never leaves this browser. No account, no tracking, no server. Built by Sebastian Poulsen

Log the week you actually had

  1. Drag down a column to mark a stretch of time, or click a single half-hour slot.
  2. Say what you were doing. Any start and end time works — 18:45 to 20:10 is fine. A category is optional; breakfast is just breakfast.
  3. Check the totals at the end of the week, then mark each category keep, compress or cut.

The bar at the top is all 168 hours of your week. The blank part is time you haven't accounted for — that gap is the point of the exercise, so resist the urge to tidy it.

Everything saves to this browser as you go. There is no save button to forget.
