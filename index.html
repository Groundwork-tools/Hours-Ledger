See where your week actually goes

  1. Drag down a column to mark a stretch of time, or click a single half-hour slot.
  2. Say what you were doing. Any start and end time works — 18:45 to 20:10 is fine. A category is optional; breakfast is just breakfast.
  3. Check the totals at the end of the week, then mark each category increase, keep or cut.

The bar at the top is all 168 hours of your week. Blank means nothing's logged there. It stays visible on purpose — resist the urge to tidy it.

Everything saves to this browser as you go. There is no save button to forget. If you later connect Google Drive to sync across your own devices, here's exactly what that touches: Privacy.
